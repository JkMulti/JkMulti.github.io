Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

The short version

Kobblo is a file manager and gallery that runs on your device. It has no accounts, no servers of ours, no ads and no analytics. We don't collect, sell or share your personal data. Your files and photos only leave your device when you choose to send them somewhere.

Files and photos on your device

Kobblo asks for All files access so it can show and manage the files, photos, videos and music on your phone. It reads, moves, copies and deletes files only when you ask it to, and does that on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Smart Search (optional)

Smart Search finds photos by what's in them, by the text they contain and by the people in them. It is off until you turn it on. When you do, Kobblo downloads the models it needs once, from Hugging Face, and checks them before use. After that, all the work happens on your phone: your photos and what is found in them never leave it.

Places

To group photos by where they were taken, Kobblo reads the location saved inside your photos, using Android's photo location permission. It matches that location against a list of towns built into the app (from GeoNames), so no location is ever sent anywhere. Kobblo never asks for your phone's own location for this.

Servers and cloud storage you connect

You can connect Kobblo to your own servers (FTP, SFTP, SMB, WebDAV and Nextcloud) and to cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox and MEGA). When you do, Kobblo talks directly from your device to that server or service. We are not in between and never see your files or sign-in details.

  • Passwords you save for servers are kept in Android's encrypted storage on your device.
  • Google, Dropbox and MEGA sign-ins happen through those services. Their own privacy policies apply to what you store with them.

Sending files: Nearby Share, Send and Web Share

Kobblo can send files directly to other devices on the same Wi-Fi network. Files go straight from one device to the other and never pass through the internet or through us.

  • Nearby Share works over your local network. While it is on, nearby devices can see the device name you set in Kobblo, and you choose whether to accept each incoming transfer.
  • Pairing can use a QR code. The camera is used only to read that code; nothing it sees is saved or sent.
  • Web Share gives you a link that a browser on the same network can open to get the files you picked. It stops when you stop sharing.

Vault and app lock

  • Files you put in the Vault are encrypted on your device, names included. Opening them takes your vault password or your recovery key. We have neither, so we can't open your Vault or recover it for you.
  • The app lock (PIN, pattern or password) is stored on your device only as a salted hash, never as the code itself.
  • If you unlock with your fingerprint or face, Android handles that check. Kobblo never receives your biometric data.

Crash reports

If Kobblo crashes, it keeps a report of the most recent crash in its private storage on your device. The report can contain file names, so it only leaves your phone if you choose to share it from the crash screen.

Permissions

  • All files access and storage: to show and manage your files.
  • Photo location: to read where photos were taken, for Places.
  • Camera: only to scan pairing QR codes.
  • Internet and network state: for the servers and cloud storage you connect, Smart Search model downloads, and sending files on your Wi-Fi.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices, Wi-Fi state and multicast: to find other devices for sending files. On Android 12 and older, Android requires the location permission for this; Kobblo doesn't use it to find where you are.
  • Notifications, foreground services and wake lock: to show progress and keep transfers, file jobs and music playing in the background.
  • See, install and remove apps: for the App Manager, and to install an APK file when you open one. Removing an app always asks you first.
  • Shizuku (optional): if you have Shizuku installed and allow it, Kobblo can reach folders that Android normally restricts.

Children

Kobblo is a general-purpose tool and is not aimed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
