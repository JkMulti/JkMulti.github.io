Privacy

Your files, all in one place.

Kobblo is a file manager for Android. Browse, organize and share your files, photos and music, on your phone and on your own servers.

Download for Android Source code

Free and open source under the GPL-3.0. Android 8.0 or later.

  • Files & galleryBrowse folders, photos, videos and documents with built-in viewers.
  • Your serversConnect to FTP, SFTP, SMB, WebDAV and Nextcloud.
  • Cloud storageOpen Google Drive, Dropbox and MEGA next to your local files.
  • Send & Web ShareSend files to devices on the same Wi-Fi, or share a link any browser can open.
  • ArchivesOpen ZIP and RAR archives, and create ZIP files.
  • Vault & app lockEncrypt private files, and lock the app with a PIN, pattern or password.
  • Smart SearchFind photos by what's in them, the text they hold, or who's in them, all on your phone.
  • MusicPlay your music library, with a queue and playback in the background.

No accounts, no tracking

Kobblo has no sign-up, no ads and no analytics. Your files stay on your device unless you choose to send them somewhere. Read the privacy policy.
