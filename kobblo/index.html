Your photos and your files, in one app.

Kobblo is a private gallery and file manager for Android. It opens your servers and cloud storage too, and nothing leaves your phone unless you send it.

Download for Android

Free and open source. Android 8.0 or later.

Every photo and video, newest first

One feed holds your whole library. Tap a photo and it grows out of its tile; flick it away and it lands back where it was.

  • Albums, favorites, On This Day and photos by town
  • Crop, rotate, straighten, filters, light and color
  • Motion photos, 360° photos and video
  • Finds similar and duplicate photos

A file manager you already know how to use

Browse your phone and SD card, drag files where you want them, and read documents without leaving the app.

  • PDF, Office, OpenDocument, EPUB and RTF documents
  • Opens ZIP and RAR archives, and creates ZIP
  • Deleted files wait in Recently Deleted
  • Cleaner, storage overview and app manager

Your server is just another folder

Add a NAS, a home server or a cloud drive once, and it sits in Browse next to everything on your phone. Kobblo connects to it directly.

  • FTP, SFTP and SMB
  • WebDAV and Nextcloud
  • Google Drive and MEGA

Send over Wi-Fi

Send to a nearby phone, pair with a code, or share a link that opens in any browser on the same network.

Search that stays home

Find photos by what's in them, the words they hold, or who's in them. After a one-time download it all runs on your phone.

Music, too

Play your library with a queue, lyrics and an equalizer, and keep listening while you do something else.

What's on your phone stays on your phone

Kobblo has no servers to send your files to. There is no sign-up, and no tracking to switch off.

No account, ads or tracking
No analytics of any kind. Open the app and start.
An encrypted Vault
Private files are encrypted, names included, behind a password of their own.
A lock for the app
PIN, pattern or password, with fingerprint or face if you like.
Open source
Licensed under the GPL-3.0, so anyone can check how it works.

Get Kobblo

  1. Download the APK from the latest release.
  2. Open it. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser.
  3. Choose what Kobblo may see, and that's it.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 or later. Smart Search, text in photos and People need Android 11 or later.

Is it on Google Play?

Not at the moment. Every release is published on GitHub, with its checksum.

Does it back up my photos?

No. Kobblo keeps your library on your phone and never uploads it on its own.

Which languages does it speak?

Sixteen, including Urdu and Arabic, which read right to left.

How do I check that an APK is genuine?

Every release is signed with the same key. Compare its certificate's SHA-256 with this one:
5C:08:20:20:0E:35:20:3C:AF:AB:C9:CD:17:E2:02:B4:D5:F6:38:C1:1D:C6:5E:A4:CF:10:28:D1:2B:46:48:99

Open an issue on GitHub, or write to bitbyteapps51@gmail.com.
